★ What Is SteinOS? ★

A Green future is a future we can live in. People, planet, peace — over profit, every time.
— The SteinOS Pledge

SteinOS — People · Planet · Peace is the movement inside the movement. Linux is the greatest operating system ever built: the kernel that powers the internet, the servers of America, the cloud, the phone in your pocket, the rocket on the pad. It was born free, built by patriots of the open source, one commit at a time. But somewhere along the way, the movement lost its soul. Bloat crept in. Corporate overlords arrived. The planet got the bill. It's time to take our kernel back — green, clean, and free.
