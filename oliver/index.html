— The OliverOS Pledge

OliverOS — Live and Let Live is the movement inside the movement. Linux is the greatest operating system ever built: the kernel that powers the internet, the servers of America, the cloud, the phone in your pocket, the rocket on the pad. It was born free, built by patriots of the open source, one commit at a time. But somewhere along the way, the movement lost its way. Bloat crept in. Corporate overlords arrived. Telemetry happened. It's time to take our kernel back — and hand it to you, fully, forever.

🔓

Fully Yours

🚫

🧭
