★ The MLGA Platform ★

  1. Build the Kernel Wall

    One commit at a time, we stop the bloat. make defconfig — the wall just got 10 feet taller.

  2. Drain the systemd Swamp

    Init freedom for every distro. You don't have to like PID 1 — and PID 1 doesn't have to own you.

  3. Make Secure Boot Great Again

    Sign your own keys. Own your own hardware. The government doesn't get a backdoor — not the big one, not the little one.

  4. America-First Boot Order

    Fast boots for the homeland. We'll boot so fast, you'll be at the login screen before the competition even posts.

  5. Git Push Liberty

    Free speech means free software. Fork the repo, push your truth, and never let a corporate license stop you.

  6. We Don't Negotiate With Malware

    Rootkits, ransomware, zero-days — we find them, we patch them, we win. Law & order for the kernel.

  7. Right to Bear Forks

    The right to fork shall not be infringed. Every distro is free to keep and bear its own repositories.

  8. Make Package Managers Great Again

    No more opaque app stores. Auditable, reproducible builds — because you should know exactly what's in your system.

  9. Terminate the GUI Overreach

    The terminal is not a crime. We celebrate the CLI warrior, the tiling window user, the .dotfiles patriot.

  10. Keep the Penguin Proud

    Linux was born free and it will die free. No corporate capture. No lock-in. Just liberty, from the bootloader to the cloud.
